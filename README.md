\# SecureNova AI — Identity Security Capstone



SecureNova AI is an AI identity security capstone project focused on threat modelling, Auth0-based identity controls, red-team attack simulation, blue-team hardening, and security/compliance documentation.



\## Project Overview



The capstone is organized into five project areas:



1\. \*\*Threat Model the AI Identity System\*\*

2\. \*\*Build and Secure the Auth0-Backed AI Application\*\*

3\. \*\*Red Team — AI Identity Attacks\*\*

4\. \*\*Blue Team — Guardrails, Hardening and Detection\*\*

5\. \*\*Policy, Compliance and Summary\*\*
